TrueCost Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

TrueCost shows what owning a car or other purchase really costs. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it. We wrote it to match exactly what the app does. "We" means TrueCost.

The short version

  • You can look up car costs and browse categories without an account. Cost estimates for other items, saving and comparing need one.
  • If you make an account, we keep your email, the settings you choose and the things you save, so they're there on any phone you sign in on.
  • There are no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and no analytics in the app. We don't sell or rent your information.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved with it at any time from the Profile tab.

What we keep when you have an account

Stored on our database host, Supabase:

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your profile: an optional name, your ZIP code, how many years you usually keep things and how many miles you drive a year. These personalize costs like sales tax, fuel prices and registration fees.
  • The cars and other items you save, with the prices and settings you entered.
  • A log of item searches you make (the search text and when), used only to enforce daily limits on new estimates.
  • The date you finished the welcome screen.

What stays only on your phone

  • Your sign-in, kept in the iPhone Keychain.
  • Your Recent list of cars and items you opened.
  • Your Light / Dark / System appearance choice.
  • Copies of public car data from the EPA, so screens load faster.

Services that receive information

TrueCost uses these companies to work. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server code), hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States: everything listed above under "What we keep".
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): when you search for an item other than a car, the search text (for example "65-inch OLED TV") is sent to Claude to estimate its costs. It's sent from our server, without your name, email or account. The estimate is then saved without any link to who asked, so anyone searching the same thing gets it instantly.
  • Resend: sends account emails (confirming your email and resetting your password), so it receives your email address and the content of those emails.
  • U.S. EPA fueleconomy.gov: when you look up a car, your phone asks this public government site for that car's details, so the site sees which car and your internet address, like any website visit.

What we don't collect

  • Your location. Costs use only the ZIP code you type in.
  • Contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone or health data.
  • Payment details. The app has no purchases.
  • Advertising identifiers. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run TrueCost: to sign you in, show costs for your area, keep what you save, send the account emails you ask for, enforce the daily estimate limits and keep the service secure. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your login, profile, saved cars and items and your item search log are deleted straight away. Our service providers may keep short-term technical logs (such as request times and internet addresses) for security and troubleshooting, which they delete on their own schedules. Saved estimates aren't linked to any person, so they aren't personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile at any time on the Profile tab.
  • Remove any saved car or item from its page.
  • Delete your whole account: Profile tab, then Delete Account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask any other privacy question, by emailing the address below. We'll answer within 30 days.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Each account can only reach its own data, enforced by the database itself. Passwords are hashed, and the keys that unlock our services stay on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users if a breach exposes it.

Children

TrueCost is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how it's used, we'll update this policy and the date at the top, and point out important changes in the app.

Contact
